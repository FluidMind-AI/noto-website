Meeting Recordings, Audio and Microphone Access

NotoNote is a meeting notes application. When you use it, we handle content that can include personal data about you and about other people who speak in your meetings. This section explains what that content is, where it goes and how you control it. It applies to the NotoNote desktop and mobile applications (the "App").

Microphone and system audio

  • The App asks your operating system for permission to use your microphone and, on desktop, to capture system audio. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your device settings.
  • The App records only after you start a recording, and it shows when a recording is in progress. The desktop App can notice that another application has started using your microphone and offer to start a recording; it does not record unless you choose to.

What we process

  • Audio recordings of your meetings;
  • Transcripts, including speaker labels and any names you assign to speakers;
  • Summaries, action items and the notes you write;
  • Meeting titles and folders, and the questions you ask about your meetings and the answers.

Where this content is processed

  • On your device. Recordings are saved on your device. On desktop, transcription and summaries can run entirely on your device; in that case the audio and the transcript do not leave it.
  • In the cloud. When you use cloud features for a meeting that is not marked private, the App sends the audio, live or as an uploaded file, to our servers. We pass it to our transcription provider (AssemblyAI, Inc., United States) to convert speech to text. We do not keep the audio file on our servers. The resulting transcript is stored in your account, and its text is processed by AI model providers that generate summaries, action items and answers to your questions.
  • Private meetings. A meeting you mark private stays on your device. It is not synced to your account and it is not sent to cloud transcription or summaries.
  • AI training. As described in "Disclosure and Transfer of Personal Data" below, your content is by default not used to train AI models.

Dictation

  • Dictation turns your speech into text that the App types where you are writing. It starts only when you press the dictation hotkey or tap the microphone button, and the App shows that it is listening. On desktop it also needs you to complete a one-time setup and grant the permissions your operating system asks for.
  • While you dictate, the audio is sent live to our servers and on to our transcription provider (AssemblyAI, Inc., United States). We do not keep the audio. The transcribed text is cleaned up by an AI model provider before it is returned to you.
  • On desktop you can choose to let dictation read nearby on-screen text so it can match spelling and tone. This is off by default, it is never read in password managers by default, and it is passed to the cleanup step only: we do not store it or write it to logs.
  • We keep a history of what you dictated (the text only) in your account, together with the custom dictionary, snippets and per-app settings you create, so you can review them. You can delete history entries, or clear them all, in the App, and deleting your account removes them.
  • Dictation is a cloud feature, so it needs an internet connection. On mobile it is unavailable while a meeting is being recorded.

Service providers

We rely on providers that process content or data on our behalf: a transcription provider (AssemblyAI, Inc.), AI model providers for summaries and answers, cloud hosting (Amazon Web Services, in the United States), payment processors (Stripe for web purchases, and Apple for purchases made in the iOS App), and analytics and crash-reporting tools used to measure feature usage and diagnose problems. These providers act under agreements with us and handle data under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

  • Recordings and transcripts on your device stay there until you delete them.
  • You can delete a meeting. Deleting it removes the transcript, summaries and notes we hold for it in your account, and the related entries used for search and answers.
  • To delete your account and the content in it, write to support@notonote.ai. We will remove your personal data from our systems except where retention is required for legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud prevention or enforcement of our agreements.

Other people in your recordings

You are responsible for telling the people you record that you are recording, and for obtaining any consent that the law where you or they are located requires. Do not record conversations where recording is not permitted.
